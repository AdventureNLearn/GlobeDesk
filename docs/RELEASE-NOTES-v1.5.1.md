# v1.5.1 Atlas

26 November 2026

GlobeDesk 1.5.1 fixes how the **Atlas** looks on a phone. Nothing else changes. The live copy is at [globedesk.grok.me](https://globedesk.grok.me).

## What's fixed

- **No more overlapping text in the Atlas on a phone.** The A–Z list, the open entry (Object, Parts, Chemistry, Sources), the Official sources, the Community links notice and the footer used to be drawn on top of each other. Now the list sits on top and the open entry sits below it. Each one scrolls on its own. Categories, By place and By year are fixed the same way.
- **A solid sheet.** The Atlas sheet, the Menu and the Layers sheet are opaque on a phone, so the globe controls behind them no longer show through the text.
- **It fits under the top bar** on small phones, so the Atlas title and the close button are not hidden.
- **Easier taps.** Source links in an entry are 44 px tall.
- **Desktop is unchanged.**

Everything else from 1.5.0 is unchanged, including the Elements periodic table and its draft data pack.
