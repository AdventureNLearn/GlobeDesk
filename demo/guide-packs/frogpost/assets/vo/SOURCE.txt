Spoken lines for the full mission were synthesized with Piper,
voice en_US-hfc_male-medium (https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices).

Clarity chain (2026-11-20): no rubberband pitch/tempo, no aecho/reverb,
gentle presence EQ at 3 kHz (+1.5 dB), lowpass 10 kHz, loudnorm -17 LUFS
(TP -2 dB, LRA 7) to avoid pumping. Output 44.1 kHz mono. Natural pitch
is preserved. See tools/make-vo.py.

The Onnx model is not stored in this folder — only the short demo WAVs.
