nasa-earth-poster.jpg is a typographic credit card, not NASA footage.

It credits the public NASA / NASA Johnson film
“Ultra High Definition (4K) View of Planet Earth (2016)”
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oFDeNcu3mnc
The background wash is from this demo's own end still (assets/a4-outro.jpg),
dimmed, not a frame of the NASA video.

The live YouTube nocookie embed is used when it plays. In recordings, and
when the embed is unavailable (for example YouTube Error 153 in headless
Chrome), the demo shows this credited still instead of an error screen.
