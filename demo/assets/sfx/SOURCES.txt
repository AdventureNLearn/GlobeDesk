Sound effects for the GlobeDesk demo.

Every file here is synthesized from scratch by demo/tools/make-sfx.py, using
ffmpeg's built-in signal generators (aevalsrc math expressions, sine tones and
seeded anoisesrc noise) and filters. No samples, recordings, sound libraries or
third-party packs are used. Re-running the script gives byte-identical files.

- ui-click.wav       1.8 kHz sine tick, fast decay
- hint-tick.wav      2.6 kHz sine tick
- ui-blip.wav        rising sine blip
- ui-boot.wav        rising sine chirp + band-passed pink noise swell
- sector-whoosh.wav  band-passed white-noise sweep
- success-chime.wav  stacked sine chime (C6, E6, G6)
- rank-up.wav        four-note sine arpeggio (C5, E5, G5, C6)
- telemetry-bed.wav  low hum, data beeps, soft brown noise (local episode audio)
- ambience-ship.wav  brown-noise rumble + slow 44 Hz sub pulse
